# Return Policy

Customers may return eligible products within 7 days of delivery.

Products must be returned in their original packaging.

All accessories included with the product must be returned.

Products damaged due to misuse are not eligible for return.

Electronics may be covered by replacement-only policies.

Damaged products should be reported within 48 hours of delivery.

Customers can initiate returns through the Orders section.

Return pickup availability depends on the delivery location.

Certain products are non-returnable due to hygiene reasons.

Refunds are processed after return verification.

Return requests may be rejected if the product is missing accessories.

The seller may inspect returned products before approving a refund.

Products must contain original tags and labels.

Replacement products are shipped after return approval.

Customers can track return requests online.
