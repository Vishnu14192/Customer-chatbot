# Shipping Policy

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days.

Express delivery is available in selected cities.

Delivery timelines depend on seller location.

Tracking information becomes available after shipment.

Customers receive shipment notifications.

Delivery delays may occur during holidays.

Large appliances may require special delivery arrangements.

Customers can track orders online.

Shipping charges depend on product category.

Some products qualify for free shipping.

Failed deliveries may be reattempted.

Shipping timelines are estimates and not guarantees.

Remote locations may require additional delivery time.

Address changes may not be possible after shipment.

Support can assist with shipping concerns.
