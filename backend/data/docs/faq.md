# Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I cancel an order?

A: Orders can be cancelled before shipment.

Q: How do I track my order?

A: Tracking information is available in the Orders section.

Q: How long does a refund take?

A: Bank refunds usually take 3 to 5 business days.

Q: Can I return a damaged product?

A: Yes, report damage within 48 hours of delivery.

Q: How do I contact support?

A: Support is available through chat, email, and phone.

Q: Can I change my delivery address?

A: Address changes may be limited after shipment.

Q: Are shipping charges refundable?

A: Shipping refunds depend on the return reason.

Q: What happens if delivery fails?

A: Delivery will usually be reattempted.
