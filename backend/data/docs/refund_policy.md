# Refund Policy

Refunds are initiated after successful return verification.

Bank account refunds typically take 3 to 5 business days.

Wallet refunds are processed within 24 hours.

UPI refunds may take up to 2 business days.

Refund timelines vary by payment method.

Refund status can be tracked in the Orders section.

Failed payments are usually reversed automatically.

Customers receive notifications when refunds are processed.

Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

Refund requests may be delayed due to bank processing times.

Gift card purchases may have different refund rules.

Partial refunds are possible for specific cases.

Refunds for cancelled orders are processed automatically.

Refund approvals depend on seller verification.

Support can be contacted for refund-related issues.
